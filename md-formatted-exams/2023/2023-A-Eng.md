 **Tips**

> **Regarding** **the** **structure**
>
> The exam consists of 8 tasks. All tasks are in long answer form. They
> are weighted as follows: 4 at 10% and 4 at 15%, but not in that order.
> The weighting is in the assignment title.
>
> **Expectations**
>
> You are expected to answer the question, discuss where relevant, but
> much of the text can be in
>
> the form of bulleted lists, tables, etc., with some explanation or
> discussion in addition. You don't need to write as many words as
> possible.
>
> In some of the assignments, references are made to long lists or
> complicated models from the lectures. In some of these, you are not
> expected to remember exact points or the entire model, but to have a
> decent understanding of it. Explain what you can, explain the
> conceptual, and don't stress that e.g. you don't remember exactly
> which communication protocols belong to which OSI model steps, etc.
